The main idea

Direct payments always have exactly two participants in the reported full scan. They also have no more than one currency conversion. This is a deterministic constraint: zero Direct-route violations were observed.

Participants are the banks involved in a payment path. An FX conversion happens when the payment changes currency.
Direct Bilateral Routing Accounts for 63.2% of Traffic vs. 32.6% Indirect & 4.2% Covered

This shows how many records use each route type. Direct is the largest group, followed by Indirect and Covered.

Direct Routes Cap FX at 1 Step, While Indirect & Covered Peak Sharply at 2 FX Steps

This compares the number of currency changes for each route type. In the reported full scan, Direct records have at most one conversion (zero violations), while Indirect and Covered commonly have two.

What we found

Verified generator relationship: Every Direct transaction has exactly 2 participants and 0 or 1 FX conversions in the reported full scan. Zero Direct-route violations were observed. Indirect and Covered transactions have more varied participant and FX counts; their most common FX count is 2. Median duration: Direct 39s, Indirect 281s, Covered 383s.

In simple words

Possible interpretation: Direct routes resemble bilateral settlement, while Indirect and Covered routes involve more participants and often more currency conversions. Multi-participant routes support more FX conversions. The synthetic fields do not independently establish the underlying account arrangements.

What to keep in mind

Deterministic constraint vs. general pattern: The Direct-route restriction (2 participants; 0–1 FX; zero violations) is a deterministic rule in the observed data. The Indirect/Covered FX distribution is a general routing pattern, not a deterministic rule. The exact percentage of routes with 2 FX conversions should be treated as chart-specific unless confirmed by the underlying data.

What we found

Observed pattern: Payment values are strongly right-skewed. FAT_FINGER_MISTAKE records have a much higher median and mean amount than the overall dataset. Romance Scam and CEO Scam have strict role restrictions

Romance Scam records are 100% consumer-originated (C2C and C2B only; zero business-originated). CEO Scam records are 100% business-originated (B2C and B2B only; zero consumer-originated). These are deterministic role restrictions in the observed data.

What we found

Verified patterns: Anomalies represent 0.0701% of all records. Romance Scam: 2,476 records — 1,811 C2C, 665 C2B, zero business-originated. In simple words

Possible interpretation: The synthetic generator appears to have role-gated rules for some anomaly categories. Romance Scam is restricted to consumer senders; CEO Scam is restricted to business senders. Anomaly median durations are compared with normal payments in the analysis.

What to keep in mind

Important caveat: These are synthetic anomaly-generation patterns, not proof of real-world fraud behaviour. The label is part of the dataset and does not independently confirm a fraud event.

Limitations and Confidence
Deterministic in observed data

Strong association

FAT_FINGER high-value pattern (21.80% exceed $10M), B2B value concentration (22.71% of payments, 72.54% of value). These are robust observed associations, not proven causal mechanisms.

Observed pattern / caveat

Duplicate-payment pattern, participant-duration relationship, country-currency relationship, banking activity distribution. These are observed patterns in the synthetic data.
